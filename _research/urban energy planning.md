To tackle the challenges of climate change, Urban Energy Planning has become more critical than ever. Our research explores ways to reduce energy use in urban space while promoting energy justice to ensure that all cities and their residents have fair access to clean, affordable energy. Our ultimate goal is to create urban environments where the benefits of sustainable energy are shared by everyone. In the age of AI, planning how we use energy in our cities is becoming even more vital. Urban energy planning is essential to manage the rising energy needs of our world and to make sure these advancements truly protect our planet.
